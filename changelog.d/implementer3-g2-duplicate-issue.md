- **Duplicate-issue flagging (hive-mind#493, G2).** `duplicate-issue.yml` runs on `issues: opened` and, for a strict duplicate (equal normalised title, body at least 95 % alike, same author, within 10 minutes), adds one comment naming the original and a `duplicate?` label. It never closes an issue, holds `issues: write` only, and reads the issue text through `env:`. Parser and rule: `scripts/ci/duplicate_issue.py`.
