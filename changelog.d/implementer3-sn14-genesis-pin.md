- **Security: a lost genesis pin is re-established automatically (SN-14).** A member device (it wrote the genesis or the owner admitted it) that has no valid `.genesis-pin` re-pins itself from the single genesis in its own journal, at the next ingest or the `hv doctor --fix` timer, with no network input; `hv doctor` now fails the `genesis` check (LOST) until it has. A device that was never a member is unchanged.
