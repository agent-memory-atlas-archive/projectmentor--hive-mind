- **Security: a lost genesis pin is re-established automatically (SN-14).** A device that wrote the genesis (the genesis carries its own device signature) and has no valid `.genesis-pin` re-pins itself from the single genesis in its own journal, at the next ingest or the `hv doctor --fix` timer, with no network input; `hv doctor` now fails the `genesis` check (LOST) until it has. Any other device, admitted ones included, is unchanged: pinning stays the operator's act.
